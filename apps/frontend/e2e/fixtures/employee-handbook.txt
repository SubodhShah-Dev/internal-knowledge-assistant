Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Annual Leave Policy
Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave requests must be
submitted at least two weeks in advance and approved by the employee's manager.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your local timezone.

Equipment
A one-time equipment stipend of 500 dollars is available to all full-time
employees upon joining the company.

Expense Reimbursement
Business travel expenses must be filed within 30 days of returning. Meals are
reimbursed up to 40 dollars per day.
